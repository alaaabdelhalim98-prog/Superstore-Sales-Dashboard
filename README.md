
Superstore Sales & Performance Dashboard



## Project Overview
This project is a comprehensive sales performance and geographical analysis dashboard based on the famous **Superstore Dataset**. The objective is to provide executive stakeholders with deep insights into revenue distribution across product categories, geographical regions, and yearly trends to optimize business strategies.

---

## Project Technologies & Tools
- **Data Source & Preparation:** Cleaned and structured transactional data.
- **Data Visualization & Dashboarding:** Microsoft Power BI.

---

## Dashboard Key Components & Visuals
1. **Executive KPI Card:** Displays the total overarching revenue (**$2.26M Sum of Sales**).
2. **Category Performance:** Bar charts highlighting top-performing product categories (*Technology, Furniture, Office Supplies*).
3. **Geographical Analysis:** An interactive map visualizing sales distribution and volume across different states.
4. **Year-Over-Year Trends:** Line charts mapping revenue growth and performance over the years (2015–2018).
5. **Interactive Filtering:** Dedicated Slicers for Categories and Regions to allow dynamic data slicing and exploration.

---

## Key Business Insights
- **Top Category:** Technology leads as the highest revenue-generating category among Superstore products.
- **Geographical Spread:** Sales concentration varies significantly across states, pointing out high-density markets for targeted marketing.
- **Growth Trajectory:** Yearly trends illustrate steady revenue progression across the operational timeframe.
