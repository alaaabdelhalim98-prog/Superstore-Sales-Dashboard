# 📊 Superstore Sales & Performance Dashboard

![Dashboard Preview](Superstore%20Dataset.png)

## 📝 Project Overview
This project is a comprehensive sales performance and geographical analysis dashboard based on the famous Superstore Dataset. The objective is to provide executive stakeholders with deep insights into revenue distribution across product categories, geographical regions, and yearly trends to optimize business strategies.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Tools
* **Database & Querying:** SQL (Data Extraction, Cleaning & Aggregation)
* **Data Visualization:** Microsoft Power BI (DAX, Interactive Modeling)

---

## 📈 Dashboard Key Components
* **Executive KPI Card:** Displays the total overarching revenue ($2.26M Total Sales).
* **Category Performance:** Bar charts highlighting top-performing product categories (Technology, Furniture, Office Supplies).
* **Geographical Analysis:** An interactive map visualizing sales distribution and volume across different states.
* **Year-Over-Year Trends:** Line charts mapping revenue growth and performance over the years (2015–2018).
* **Interactive Filtering:** Dedicated Slicers for Categories and Regions for dynamic data exploration.

---

## 💡 Key Business Insights
* **Top Category:** Technology leads as the highest revenue-generating category among Superstore products.
* **Geographical Spread:** Sales concentration varies significantly across states, pointing out high-density markets for targeted marketing.
* **Growth Trajectory:** Yearly trends illustrate steady revenue progression across the operational timeframe.
